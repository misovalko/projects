Course Description

The graphs come handy whenever we deal with relations between the objects. This course, focused on learning, presents methods involving two main sources of graphs in ML: graphs coming from networks, such as social, biological and technology networks, and graphs constructed from flat data, where a graph serves as a useful nonparametric basis for tasks such as spectral clustering, manifold learning and semi-supervised learning. We also discuss online decision-making on graphs, suitable for recommender systems and online advertising, together with scalability to very large graphs.

The lectures show not only how but mostly why things work. Students learn relevant topics from spectral graph theory, learning theory, bandit theory and graph neural networks, together with the mathematical concepts behind graph-based machine learning. Practical sessions provide hands-on experience with applications and graph-processing tools.
