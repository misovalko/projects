Graphical Models, Exponential Families, and Variational Inference
Wainwright, M.J. & Jordan, M.I.
2003
Technical Report 649, UC Berkeley
📄 PDF

Abstract

The formalism of probabilistic graphical models provides a unifying framework for the development of large-scale multivariate statistical models. Working with exponential family representations, and exploiting the conjugate duality between the cumulant generating function and the entropy, we develop general variational representations of the problems of computing marginal probabilities and modes.
